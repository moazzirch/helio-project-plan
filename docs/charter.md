# Project Charter: HELIO

**Project title:** HELIO – AI-Powered Chatbot Development for Customer Support Automation (student-services case)

**Mission:** Reduce friction in accessing routine student support while keeping advice current, attributable, accessible and subject to human correction.

**Measurable organisational value (MOV):** A sustained reduction in avoidable repeat enquiries without a rise in material guidance errors. Baselines are measured during discovery.

## Key parameters

| Dimension | Position |
|---|---|
| Sponsor | Student-services executive; signs charter, budget, policy gates and final acceptance |
| Business owner | Student Services Manager; accountable for answer content and operating handover |
| Start / finish | 2 Nov 2026 – 5 Mar 2027 (18 calendar weeks) |
| Cost baseline | AUD 300,000 including AUD 50,000 risk contingency |
| In scope | Verified-answer knowledge base; web interface; accessible triage; permissioned read-only LMS/SMS lookups; human handoff; logging; quality testing; staff enablement; monitored pilot |
| Out of scope | Autonomous fee, enrolment, grading, visa or legal decisions; record modifications; unrestricted external browsing; training general AI models on student data |
| Dependencies | Approved source content; vendor data terms; sandbox API access; availability of agents, testers and reviewers |
| Acceptance | Business, privacy/security and sponsor sign-off against gates G1–G6 |

## Approval gates

- G1: Charter and evidence plan (end W1)
- G2: Privacy/vendor decision and approved sources (end W6)
- G3: Integrated demonstration (end W12)
- G4: Independent security and accessibility gate (W15)
- G5: Business UAT (W16)
- G6: Sponsor release acceptance after the W18 pilot
